## Classification key

- **AI-generated:** an AI tool created the actual text, image, or translation. KDP disclosure is required for affected content.
- **AI-assisted:** the author created the content and AI was used for brainstorming, editing, refinement, or error-checking. KDP says disclosure is not required for this category, although the author remains responsible for the result.
- **Human-created:** no generative AI created or materially transformed the content.
